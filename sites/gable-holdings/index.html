Questions borrowers and brokers ask

What is Gable Holdings?

Gable Holdings, LLC is a non-bank lender at 205 Worth Avenue in Palm Beach, Florida. Its account executives arrange residential and commercial real estate loans for investors, builders and brokers across multiple states. It is a Wyoming LLC registered in Florida since October 2015, managed by Weston E. Spicer.

Is Gable Holdings a bank?

No. Gable Holdings is a non-bank lender. It does not take deposits, and loans are not FDIC-insured bank products. Every loan is subject to underwriting and approval.

What loans does Gable Holdings arrange?

Residential and commercial real estate loans of all types: hard money, fix-and-flip, bridge, ground-up construction, DSCR rental, bank-statement, commercial bridge and commercial permanent loans.

Which states does Gable Holdings lend in?

Gable Holdings arranges multi-state lending. Ask the lending desk about your property’s state and an account executive will confirm whether it fits.

How do I start a loan request?

Tell the 24/7 lending desk on this page about your deal, by typing or by voice. It asks a few questions, checks the basics and sends your file to an account executive, who follows up directly.

Can mortgage brokers work with Gable Holdings?

Yes. Brokers can submit deals through the lending desk, and partner brokers can add it to their own websites with one line of code so every lead they send is credited to them.
